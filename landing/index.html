Getting Started

Up and running in 2 minutes

No bank account needed. Just your phone number.

1
Download the App

Install Synced from the Google Play Store on your Android phone.

2
Sign Up with Your Phone

Enter your Ugandan number, receive an OTP, and you're in. No email required.

3
Add Your Expenses

Log spending manually or sync via MTN MoMo SMS. Transactions are auto-categorized.

4
Invite Your Household

Share an invite code with family members. Create a shared household wallet together.
